Who can play (eligibility)

Checked against the player list when players register. A player who doesn't qualify is told why and how to fix it; anything the list can't answer goes to Pending verification for you.

In that column, Qualified (already earned their spot) and Not Eligible (not 10 lifetime matches in the format yet) can't enter, and each player is told why.
