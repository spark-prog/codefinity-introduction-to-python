Director's guide

Tournament night

Everything the director does, in order. The numbers below come from this tournament's current settings.

Back to the director page
1. Days before 2. At the venue 3. Start 4. Running matches 5. Cash-in 6. Last call 7. Paying out 8. Fixing mistakes 9. After the night

1. Days before

  1. Set a director password. Start the app with ADMIN_PASSWORD=your-password python app.py. Without it, the password is admin.
  2. Turn on real texts (optional). Set the three Twilio settings described in the README. Until then, texts are only logged under Text alerts → Recent texts.
  3. Fill in Settings on the Director page:
    • Name, date, start time, venue, address, and details for players. These show on the home page with a map link.
    • Entry fee and added money. These can still change after the start, until every team has completed a match.
    • Field cap: the most teams in the event. Teams that register after it's full go on the waiting list.
    • Tables, as a comma-separated list using the names on the tables (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 7).
    • Race to (now {{ s.race_to }}).
    • Chip brackets and the registration cap (now {{ s.registration_cap }}). These lock once every team has completed a match.
    • Check-in times: warm-up {{ s.warmup_minutes }} min, check-in {{ s.checkin_minutes }} min, grace {{ s.grace_minutes }} min.
    • Cash-in and last call times. These are reminders on screen only; nothing happens until you press the button.
  4. Post the rules with Edit rules on the Director page: paste each game's rules (8-Ball, 9-Ball, Fast 8, Fast Saratoga). Players read and search them under Rules in the menu.
  5. Share the registration link (/register). Teams get a text with their 4-digit PIN for roster changes. Teams that signed up some other way, such as a Google Form, can be brought in with Import teams from a spreadsheet (CSV) on the Director page. Upload the responses, check the preview, and import.
  6. Players without a partner can register alone to hold a spot. They must add a partner by {{ t.partner_deadline_text() }}; at that time any team in the field still without a partner moves to the waiting list automatically. To team up two of them, use Pair next to either one in Teams & chips.
  7. Send yourself a test text. Register a practice team with your own cell number, check that the text arrives, then remove the team.

2. At the venue (about an hour before)

  1. Put the Board on the TV: open /board in a browser. It refreshes itself every 15 seconds.
  2. Check teams in as they arrive{% if s.checkin_by %} (check-in is by {{ s.checkin_by }}){% endif %}, and collect entry fees ({{ s.entry_fee_cents|money }} per team). Press Check in next to the team in the Teams & chips table; the time is recorded and the count at the top updates. Use the search box above the table (team name, player name or last 4 of a cell) to find a team fast, and tick Not checked in only to see who's still missing. Waiting-list teams who show up can be checked in too.
  3. Look for red "needs new partner" notes. A team over a combined {{ t.max_skill }} can't start. Have it swap partners (the new pair must be {{ s.registration_cap }} or less) or remove it.
  4. Mark no-shows with No-show, or once check-in closes, use Mark all not checked in as no-shows. Each no-show gets a text, and the next team on the waiting list moves into the field and gets a text (check it in when it arrives). Restore undoes a no-show: the team goes back into the field if there's room, otherwise to the end of the waiting list. Raising the field cap also moves waiting teams in.
  5. Start stays greyed out until every team in the field is checked in or marked a no-show. You can turn that requirement off in Settings.
  6. Roster questions: click Roster next to any team to edit it without a PIN. Each team's PIN is shown under its name if it forgot.

3. Starting the tournament

Press Start tournament. The app:

  • adds up the pot (entry fees + added money), with waiting-list teams not included
  • gives each team its chips based on combined skill
  • puts every team on the winners list in random order and calls the top two to each free table, texting both teams the match-up

The app won't start if fewer than 2 teams are in the field, no tables are set, or any team is over {{ t.max_skill }}. The error message names the teams.

4. Running matches

Each match on a table has a card on the Director page:

Pairing is rolling, so there's nothing to do between matches. When a match ends, the winner goes to the bottom of the winners list and the loser to the bottom of the losers list. A team joining a list is compared with the top team on that list, then the second, and so on, and is paired with the first one it hasn't played yet. The pair goes into Teams waiting on a table, and pairs are sent to free tables fewest rounds played first. Both teams get a text with the match-up when they're called.

  • No rematches unless it's the only option. A team that has already played everyone waiting on its list is tagged waiting for a new opponent and holds until another team arrives, even if a table sits free. A rematch happens only when both teams have played everyone still in, or when nothing is playing or queued that could bring a new opponent. Rematches are tagged in the queue.
  • Winners vs losers matches only happen when teams are stuck on opposite lists with nothing else coming, or for a team that's catching up.
  • Catching up: a team {{ s.catchup_matches }} or more matches behind the leader (after arriving late, or when an opponent cashes in) is tagged catching up and moves to the top of its list. If no one else is waiting on its list, it plays the top team of the other list.
  • Call next: on a pair in the queue, it moves that pair to the front for the next free table. On a team still on a list, it pairs that team right away (a new opponent from either list if one is waiting, otherwise a rematch) and puts the pair first. Use it when a team has been holding too long.
  • Slow play: each card shows how long the match has been playing. It turns amber after {{ s.match_minutes }} minutes and red at {{ s.long_match_minutes }} minutes past the start, and a "Running long" banner lists those tables. Go speak to the table. Catch-up can't help a team that plays slowly every match; only speeding them up can.

Late teams can register (or a no-show can be restored) until every team has completed a match. They pay the entry fee, get their chips and join the winners list, or go on the waiting list if the field is full. Until that same moment you can still change the entry fee, added money, chip brackets and registration cap in Settings; the pot and teams' chips adjust to match (see the chip ledger). Once every team has played, a banner says entries are locked, registration closes, and waiting-list teams get a text that the field is set.

5. Cash-in{% if s.cash_in_time %} (scheduled {{ s.cash_in_time }}){% endif %}

  1. Press Open cash-in. Every team gets a text with the chip value and what its chips are worth.
  2. When a team comes to the desk, press Cash in $… in its row. Pay the amount shown in the green message.
  3. A team can't cash in once a game in its current match has been scored. If its match hasn't started, the match is cancelled and the opponent goes back to the front of the line.
  4. Other teams' chips are worth the same after a cash-in. Chip value only goes up when a chip goes back to the table.

6. Last call{% if s.last_call_time %} (scheduled {{ s.last_call_time }}){% endif %}

Press Last call. Every team gets a text, and the Board shows a banner.

  • Teams not on a table play one more match. When it ends, both teams are cashed in automatically.
  • Teams already on a table finish that match, then play one more, then are cashed in.
  • The Teams & chips table shows "last match" or "2 matches left" for each team.
  • A team with nobody left to play is cashed in right away. Registration closes.
  • The tournament finishes on its own when the last team is cashed in.

Emergency only: End now — cash everyone out stops every match and cashes every team in at once (for example, if the venue is closing). It can be reversed with Undo.

7. Paying out

  • The Value / paid column shows what each cashed-in team is owed. The green message after each cash-in shows the same amount.
  • The chip check line under the team table confirms chips issued = in play + returned + cashed in, and money collected = paid out + pot left. At the end, the pot left should be $0.00.
  • Every chip movement, with its reason, is in the Chip ledger under Activity log.

9. After the night

  • Make sure every team in Teams & chips shows as cashed out or eliminated, and the pot left is $0.00.
  • To run another tournament, create it from All tournaments. To reuse this one, type RESET under Start over. Archive and start over keeps the finished tournament under Past tournaments. Delete permanently and start over erases it, with its phone numbers and texts.
  • Archived tournaments still hold player names and phone numbers. Delete them under Past tournaments once you've settled up.
