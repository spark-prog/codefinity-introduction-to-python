Tournament night: director's guide

Everything the director does, in order. The numbers below come from this tournament's current settings.

1. Days before · 2. At the venue · 3. Start · 4. Running matches · 5. Cash-in · 6. Last call · 7. Paying out · 8. Fixing mistakes · 9. After the night

1. Days before

  1. Set a director password. Start the app with ADMIN_PASSWORD=your-password python app.py. Without it, the password is admin.
  2. Turn on real texts (optional). Set the three Twilio settings described in the README. Until then, texts are only logged under Text alerts → Recent texts.
  3. Fill in Settings on the Director page:
    • Name, date, start time, venue, address, and details for players. These show on the home page with a map link.
    • Entry fee and added money. These lock when you press Start.
    • Field cap: the most teams in the event. Teams that register after it's full go on the waiting list.
    • Tables, as a comma-separated list using the names on the tables (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 7).
    • Race to (now {{ s.race_to }}).
    • Chip brackets and the registration cap (now {{ s.registration_cap }}). These lock at the start.
    • Check-in times: warm-up {{ s.warmup_minutes }} min, check-in {{ s.checkin_minutes }} min, grace {{ s.grace_minutes }} min.
    • Cash-in and last call times. These are reminders on screen only; nothing happens until you press the button.
  4. Share the registration link (/register). Teams get a text with their 4-digit PIN for roster changes. Teams that signed up some other way, such as a Google Form, can be brought in with Import teams from a spreadsheet (CSV) on the Director page. Upload the responses, check the preview, and import.
  5. Send yourself a test text. Register a practice team with your own cell number, check that the text arrives, then remove the team.

2. At the venue (about an hour before)

  1. Put the Board on the TV: open /board in a browser. It refreshes itself every 15 seconds.
  2. Check in teams and collect entry fees ({{ s.entry_fee_cents|money }} per team). Use the Teams & chips table on the Director page.
  3. Look for red "needs new partner" notes. A team over a combined {{ t.max_skill }} can't start. Have it swap partners (the new pair must be {{ s.registration_cap }} or less) or remove it.
  4. Remove no-shows with Remove. The next team on the waiting list moves into the field and gets a text. Raising the field cap does the same.
  5. Roster questions: click Roster next to any team to edit it without a PIN. Each team's PIN is shown under its name if it forgot.

3. Starting the tournament

Press Start tournament. The app:

  • adds up the pot (entry fees + added money), with waiting-list teams not included
  • gives each team its chips based on combined skill
  • draws round 1 at random and calls matches to free tables, texting each team its table

The app won't start if fewer than 2 teams are in the field, no tables are set, or any team is over {{ t.max_skill }}. The error message names the teams.

4. Running matches

Each match on a table has a card on the Director page:

WhenWhat you doWhat the app does
A team reaches its tableTap Arrived for that team.Shows a ✓ and the time.
Both teams are thereNothing.Sets the start time {{ s.warmup_minutes }} min later and texts both teams.
A team isn't there {{ s.checkin_minutes }} min after being calledNothing (the Arrived button turns red).Starts a {{ s.grace_minutes }}-minute grace period and texts the team its forfeit time.
The team arrives during graceTap Arrived.Cancels the forfeit. The start is {{ s.warmup_minutes }} min later.
Grace runs outNothing.Records a forfeit: the no-show gives a chip back and the opponent wins. If neither team showed, both give a chip back and go to the losers list.
Each game is wonTap + for that team (− fixes a slip).Entering a score also counts both teams as checked in.
A team reaches the raceNothing, or tap Winner to end a match early.Ends the match, takes a chip from the loser, updates the chip value and texts both teams.

Between rounds there's nothing to do. When the last match of a round ends, the app pairs winner 1 vs winner 2, loser 1 vs loser 2, and so on, and calls the matches to tables. An odd winner plays an odd loser. A single team left over gets a bye and keeps its chips. If there are more matches than tables, the extras wait and are called as tables free up.

Late teams can register until last call. They pay the entry fee, get their chips and join the winners list, or go on the waiting list if the field is full.

5. Cash-in{% if s.cash_in_time %} (scheduled {{ s.cash_in_time }}){% endif %}

  1. Press Open cash-in. Every team gets a text with the chip value and what its chips are worth.
  2. When a team comes to the desk, press Cash in $… in its row. Pay the amount shown in the green message.
  3. A team can't cash in once a game in its current match has been scored. If its match hasn't started, the match is cancelled and the opponent waits for the next round.
  4. Other teams' chips are worth the same after a cash-in. Chip value only goes up when a chip goes back to the table.

6. Last call{% if s.last_call_time %} (scheduled {{ s.last_call_time }}){% endif %}

Press Last call. Every team gets a text, and the Board shows a banner.

  • Teams not on a table play one more match. When it ends, both teams are cashed in automatically.
  • Teams already on a table finish that match, then play one more, then are cashed in.
  • The Teams & chips table shows "last match" or "2 matches left" for each team.
  • A team with nobody left to play is cashed in right away. Registration closes.
  • The tournament finishes on its own when the last team is cashed in.

Emergency only: End now — cash everyone out stops every match and cashes every team in at once (for example, if the venue is closing). It can be reversed with Undo.

7. Paying out

  • The Value / paid column shows what each cashed-in team is owed. The green message after each cash-in shows the same amount.
  • The chip check line under the team table confirms chips issued = in play + returned + cashed in, and money collected = paid out + pot left. At the end, the pot left should be $0.00.
  • Every chip movement, with its reason, is in the Chip ledger under Activity log.

8. Fixing mistakes

ProblemFix
Wrong winner, wrong score, or any button pressed by mistakeUndo reverses the most recent action; press again to go further back (up to 100 steps). The button shows what it will undo. Texts already sent can't be recalled, so send a correction with Text every team.
A forfeit went through but the team was therePress Undo. The app pauses the auto-forfeit on that match; then tap Arrived.
A team's chip count is wrong and Undo would go back too farUse Adjust in the team's row (e.g. +1 or -1) and enter a reason. Adjusting to 0 eliminates the team.
A team forgot its PINIt's shown under the team name in Teams & chips.
A team didn't get a textCheck Text alerts → Recent texts for the number and status, then fix the number with Roster.
A table goes out of serviceRemove it from Tables in Settings once its match is finished. A table with a match on it can't be removed.
Need to announce somethingUse Text every team (e.g. "15-minute break, back at 9:45").
The laptop restarts or the app closesNothing is lost; everything is saved after each action. Start the app again and open the Director page. The check-in clock resumes once a page is opened.

9. After the night

  • Make sure every team in Teams & chips shows as cashed out or eliminated, and the pot left is $0.00.
  • To set up the next tournament, type RESET under New tournament and press the button. The finished tournament is archived (not deleted) in the app's data folder.
